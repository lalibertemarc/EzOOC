# EzOOC

## Unreleased

- **Out of character, in brackets.** `/ooc <text>` says `(( <text> ))`; `/ooc p`, `y`, `g`, `o`, `r`, `e` and `w <name>` put it in party, yell, guild, officer, raid, an emote or a whisper. Brackets you type yourself aren't doubled, and `/ooc spaces off` writes `((like this))`.
- **A box of your own.** A key binding (Options > Keybindings > EzOOC) or `/ooc box` opens a small edit box, like EzAccent's: Enter says the line in `(( ))`, the channel shows at the start of the line, `/p ` or `/w bob ` switches it, Tab whispers the next person it knows.
- **Answer in one click.** Other people's OOC lines (`((...))`, `OOC:` or `[OOC]`) get a `[Reply]` link: click it and the box opens on their channel, or a whisper to them. A line from the last two minutes you haven't answered opens the box on them by itself, and `/oocr <text>` answers the last one straight away. `/ooc answer whisper` always answers in a whisper.
